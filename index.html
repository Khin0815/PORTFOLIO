About

I'm John Kenneth Garcenila, a front-end developer who turns designs into fast, accessible websites that work on every screen.

A front-end developer builds everything people see and touch on a website: layout, color, motion, buttons and forms. It is where design, code and empathy meet.

That is what inspires me. One small change in spacing or contrast can make a page easier for someone to use, so I keep learning, keep building and keep the details sharp.
